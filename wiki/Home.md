# ThoughtKnot

**ThoughtKnot turns what you know into a map you can see, change and share.**

You write things and the links between them as plain text. ThoughtKnot draws them, checks them and keeps them safe. It
works across your notes, your devices and your team.

## One product, many ways in

The same core sits under every way in, so a graph looks and behaves the same everywhere.

| Way in | For whom | State |
|---|---|---|
| **Obsidian plugin** | People who keep notes in Obsidian | In testing |
| **Standalone app** (desktop and iPhone) | Anyone, with no other tool needed | Planned |
| **VS Code plugin** | Developers, beside their code | Planned |
| **JavaScript library** | Builders who want graphs in their own tools | Included in each release |
| **Plug-ins** | Anyone adding new views, checks or stores | Planned |

## Where it is going

- **A mesh of notes.** Graphs in different notes join into one picture.
- **Trust you can see.** How sure a link is, who added it and when.
- **Views for each reader.** The same graph, shown at the right level for each audience.
- **Safe AI help.** AI suggests changes, within the permissions you set. You accept or reject each one.
- **Private sync.** Your devices and your team share graphs directly and encrypted.

The [Roadmap](Roadmap) shows when each part lands.
