# Code of conduct

ThoughtKnot is made by people from many places, languages and backgrounds. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull
requests, discussions, reviews or the wiki, is treated with respect.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, above all with people who are new or who write in a second language.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Disagree with an idea by saying what you would do instead.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Use plain words. Explain a term the first time you use it.
- Accept a decision once it is made, and say so if you still think it is wrong.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, threats, or comments about someone's body, race, religion, gender, sexuality, age, disability or nationality.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private, including unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Sharing someone's private details without their permission.
- Sexual language or images.
- Repeatedly derailing a conversation, or pushing others out of it.

## How to report

Tell the maintainer in private: use the email address on the
[@rogerbucks](https://github.com/rogerbucks) GitHub profile, or GitHub's own
[report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) form. A
report is read in confidence, and the person who made it is never named without their permission.

## What happens next

The maintainer may edit or remove a comment, lock a conversation, or ask, warn or block a person, depending on what
happened and whether it has happened before. The person is told why.

This code applies everywhere the project meets people: its repository, its wiki, its discussions, and anywhere someone
speaks for it.
